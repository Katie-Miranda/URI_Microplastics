How to Store Your Birds

📦 Before Pickup — Please Do the Following

Place each bird in its own ziploc/trash/paper bag or other container and freeze as soon as possible after harvest. Carcasses can be whole or breasted-out. Multiple birds from a single hunt can go in a single bag.

Label each bag with the following information:

  1. Your name
  2. Your phone number
  3. Species
  4. Harvest date
  5. Harvest time (approximate)
  6. Harvest location (nearest town and distance from it, OR GPS coordinates)

Please keep birds frozen until pickup. No freezer room? Please contact us directly to coordinate a pickup as soon as possible — email katie.miranda@uri.edu or call (985) 231‑8337. We're happy to be flexible!
